# Zooz Creators

Welcome to the **Zooz Creators** workspace. This directory contains the guidelines, monetization agreements, and visual design assets for creators, writers, and influencers on the Zooz platform.

## Highlights
- **Creator Studio**: Mockups/guides for managing post reach, performance statistics, watch-time, and audience growth analytics.
- **Multi-Asset Ad Boost & Promotion Hub**:
  - Boost Reels & Short Videos
  - Boost High-Res Photos & Banner Flyers
  - Boost Third-Party Social Platforms (YouTube, Spotify, Instagram, TikTok, Website)
  - Boost Affiliate Products (Amazon Associates, TikTok Shop, Shopify with custom promo codes)
- **Dynamic Reach & Views Estimator**:
  - Live CPM calculation predicting estimated impressions, clicks, and daily delivery pace tailored to user coin budget ($0.05 / Coin).
- **Live Feed Visibility & Preview**:
  - Boosted campaigns immediately display in the "For You" FYP pager, in the dedicated "Promotions" top sub-tab, and in Explore search.
  - Creators can preview and test external links directly from the Creator Tools ad boost manager.
- **Monetization Engine**: Direct gift tipping (Diamonds), subscription tiers, and withdrawal payout logs (PayPal, Bank Transfer, Stripe).
- **Collabs & Sponsorships**: Tools to link with brands, merchants, and affiliate marketplaces seamlessly on the platform.
